## Design Specifications

### Visual Identity
- **Primary background:** Dark charcoal (#1a1a2e) or dark navy (#16213e)
- **Accent color:** Copper/bronze (#b87333) -- for key numbers, highlights, borders
- **Secondary accent:** Zinc silver (#c0c0c0) -- for secondary data, dividers
- **Text:** White on dark backgrounds, system fonts or Montserrat/Open Sans
- **Numbers:** Extra-large bold typography for headline stats ($2.2M, 1,440 TM, 936 rods)

### Photography
- **Real plant photos ONLY** -- no stock imagery
- **Source photos from:** Andres Montbrun's phone/collection (ask him for plant photos if not in repo)
- **Usage:** Full-bleed on cover + section dividers, inset on content slides
- **If no plant photos available yet:** Use solid dark backgrounds with accent lines. Leave photo placeholders marked clearly.

### Slide Dimensions
- 16:9 widescreen
- Must work for: screen presentation, Zoom share, PDF email, and print

### Data Visualizations (slides 10-13)
- Bar charts, waterfall charts in brand colors
- Tables: clean, dark background, copper headers
- No 3D effects, no gradients -- flat, professional, industrial
