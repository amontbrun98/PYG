### Galvanizing Revenue Model

Revenue is billed per kg of galvanized output. Using $1.50/kg as conservative base (30% margin compliance):

| Item | Calculation | Per Ton |
|---|---|---|
| Variable cost | Direct costs | $345 |
| Zinc absorption cost | 10% x $4,220/TM = $422/ton galvanized | $422 |
| Total variable cost per ton | | $767 |
| Revenue at 30% margin | $767 / 0.70 = $1,096/ton | $1,096 |
| Revenue at market rate ($1,500/ton) | | $1,500 |
| Gross profit at market rate | $1,500 - $767 | $733/ton (49% margin) |

**Note:** We use $1,500/ton ($1.50/kg) as the conservative revenue figure for projections. This is within the 30% regulated margin when calculated on the full cost basis (variable + allocated fixed). Higher market rates ($1.70/kg) are achievable but not assumed in the base case.

## Risk Factors and Mitigants

| Risk | Impact | Mitigant |
|---|---|---|
| Zinc price volatility | Cost increase | Zinc is a pass-through cost -- client pays based on absorption rate. LME 2026 forecast: $2,900-$3,400/TM range. |
| Regulatory margin cap (30%) | Revenue ceiling | Market pricing achieves ~50% actual margin. 30% is the legal floor, not the ceiling in practice. |
| Client volume below projections | Lower revenue | Break-even at only 21 TM/month (7% of conservative capacity). 6 clients already expressing interest. |
| Equipment failure | Downtime | $25K-$50K maintenance budget allocated. Ing. Miriam's 36 years of experience. Burners verified GOOD. |
| Currency/exchange risk | Value erosion | Revenue denominated in USD-equivalent pricing. Client billing follows USD exchange rate. |
| Political/regulatory risk | Operating disruption | Post-January 2026 government is oil-sector friendly. PYGLARA serves infrastructure (bipartisan demand). |
| PDVSA gas supply interruption | Cannot heat kettles | Active contract. Gas is subsidized and reliably supplied to industrial users. Electric backup feasible. |

## Conclusion

Under even the most conservative assumptions (Scenario A), PYGLARA generates a **163% return on investment in Year 1** with a **7.4-month payback period**. The break-even volume of 21 TM/month represents just 7% of proven capacity -- meaning PYGLARA is profitable from virtually the first month of operation.

The $1,000,000 asking price represents a **54-67% discount to replacement cost**. An investor who acquires PYGLARA and invests $263K-$336K in startup materials begins generating $171K+/month in operating profit within one quarter.
