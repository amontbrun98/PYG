**Conclusions:**
1. **Reforma tributaria anunciada por presidencia** — no date, no details, no implementation plan. Could affect: ISLR rates, IVA rate, parafiscal contribution percentages, IGTF, or the ajuste por inflación mechanism. Entirely unknown scope.
2. **Aumento de salario mínimo** — if the minimum wage increases, parafiscal base calculations increase proportionally. IVSS, BANAVIH, and LOPCYMAT contributions are all indexed to minimum wage. Also affects labor cost projections at full operation.
3. **INCES exemption threshold** — Geraldine flagged this specifically. If the reform redefines the worker threshold for INCES obligations, PYGLARA could lose its current exemption once it grows beyond 1 formal worker.
4. **Watch flag only** — no action required now. Monitor official SENIAT and Gaceta Oficial announcements. Nothing to model until specifics are published.
