# PYGLARA — Prensados y Galvanizados de Lara, S.A.

## Project Overview

This repository contains all documentation, financial models, and investor materials for the acquisition opportunity of **Prensados y Galvanizados de Lara, S.A. (PYGLARA)**, a turnkey hot-dip galvanizing and copper electroplating facility in Barquisimeto, Venezuela.

## Two Revenue Lines

### 1. Hot-Dip Galvanizing (Primary)
- **Capacity:** 400 tons/month (4,800 tons/year)
- **Model:** Service-only — clients bring their steel, PYG galvanizes it
- **Kettles:** 7m Pilling (installed, waiting for zinc), 9m Pilling (pending install, valued $570K), 65cm centrifuge (ACTIVE — galvanizing nails), 3m (inactive — salvaged from 7m, needs refractory reline)
- **Manufacturer:** W. Pilling Riepe GmbH & Co. KG (Germany)
- **Pricing:** Based on 10% zinc absorption rate, charged by weight

### 2. Copper Electroplating (Secondary)
- **Capacity:** 936 ground rods/day (24hr continuous cycle)
- **Product:** Copper-coated ground rods, 5/8" to 1" diameter, 1.2m to 3m length
- **Coating:** 300 um +/- 50 copper thickness
- **Revenue potential:** $561K-$1.05M/year at full capacity
