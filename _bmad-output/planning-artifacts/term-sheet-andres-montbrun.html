2. Title and Role Non-Negotiable

Andres Montbrun shall be formally appointed General Manager of PYGLARA upon signing of this agreement. This title shall appear in all company records and in communications with clients, suppliers, banking institutions, and government entities.

3. Compensation

3a. Base Salary Negotiable — Floor: $3,000/month USD

Base salary is deferred until the first commercial order ships from the plant. Upon that event, a monthly base salary of no less than $3,000 USD shall be paid to Andres Montbrun.

This structure protects the company's capital during the reactivation phase while ensuring the General Manager is compensated as soon as the company generates revenue. The salary floor reflects the scope of the role: web development, branding, commercial development, supplier management, team organization, banking coordination, and corporate administration — functions that would cost $4,800–$8,200/month if hired separately.

3b. Revenue-Linked Bonus Negotiable — Floor: 3%

Once the company exceeds $50,000 USD in monthly revenue, Andres Montbrun shall receive a monthly performance bonus equal to 3% of gross company revenue in addition to base salary. This applies to all revenue streams under his operational management.

4. Equity Participation Milestones Negotiable — Total Floor: 6%

Equity shall vest in tranches tied to operational milestones achieved under Andres Montbrun's management. Total equity granted shall be no less than 6.0% of the company's outstanding shares.

Milestone Equity Tranche Cumulative
First $100,000 in cumulative orders shipped 1.5% 1.5%
First $500,000 in cumulative orders shipped 1.5% 3.0%
$1,500,000 annual revenue run rate achieved 2.0% 5.0%
$3,000,000 annual revenue run rate achieved 1.0% 6.0%
Total 6.0%

Anti-Dilution Protection Non-Negotiable

Any future capital raise or shareholder event shall not reduce Andres Montbrun's equity percentage without his written consent. In the event of dilution, Andres Montbrun shall retain a pro-rata right to maintain his ownership percentage.

5. Change of Control Non-Negotiable

In the event that PYGLARA is sold, transferred, or acquired — in whole or in part — while Andres Montbrun is actively serving as General Manager, he shall receive 5% of the gross sale price, independent of and in addition to any vested equity at the time of sale.

Andres Montbrun's contributions directly increase the operational value and commercial attractiveness of the company. This clause ensures his participation in any realized exit is protected regardless of vesting status at the time of sale.

6. Term and General Conditions

This agreement becomes effective upon signing by all parties. Either party may terminate the management arrangement with 60 days written notice. Upon termination for any reason other than cause, all vested equity tranches are retained by Andres Montbrun. Unvested tranches are forfeited upon voluntary resignation or termination for cause. This term sheet shall be formalized into a binding shareholders agreement within 30 days of signing.
