## 1. TITLE AND ROLE *(Non-Negotiable)*

Andres Montbrun shall be formally appointed **General Manager** of PYGLARA upon signing of this agreement. This title shall appear in company records and in any communications with clients, suppliers, banking institutions, and government entities.

---

## 2. COMPENSATION

### 2a. Base Salary *(Negotiable — Floor: $3,000/month USD)*

Base salary is deferred until the **first commercial galvanizing or electroplating order ships from the plant.** Upon that event, a monthly base salary of no less than **$3,000 USD** shall be paid to Andres Montbrun. This salary shall be reviewed upward at each revenue milestone defined in Section 2b.

*Rationale: This structure protects the company's capital during the reactivation phase while ensuring that the General Manager is compensated as soon as the company generates revenue.*

### 2b. Revenue-Linked Bonus *(Negotiable — Floor: 3% of gross)*

In addition to base salary, Andres Montbrun shall receive a monthly performance bonus equal to **3% of gross company revenue** once the company exceeds $50,000 USD in monthly revenue. This applies to all revenue streams under his operational management.

---

## 3. EQUITY PARTICIPATION *(Milestone Percentages Negotiable — Total Floor: 6%)*

Equity shall vest in tranches tied to operational milestones achieved under Andres Montbrun's management. Total equity granted shall be no less than **6.0%** of the company's outstanding shares.

| Milestone | Equity Tranche | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| First $100,000 in cumulative orders shipped | 1.5% | 1.5% |
| First $500,000 in cumulative orders shipped | 1.5% | 3.0% |
| $1,500,000 annual revenue run rate achieved | 2.0% | 5.0% |
| $3,000,000 annual revenue run rate achieved | 1.0% | 6.0% |

**Anti-Dilution Protection *(Non-Negotiable):*** Any future capital raise or shareholder event shall not reduce Andres Montbrun's equity percentage without his written consent. In the event of dilution, Andres Montbrun shall be granted a pro-rata right to maintain his ownership percentage.

---

## 4. CHANGE OF CONTROL CLAUSE *(Non-Negotiable)*

In the event that PYGLARA is sold, transferred, or acquired — in whole or in part — while Andres Montbrun is actively serving as General Manager, he shall receive **5% of the gross sale price**, independent of and in addition to any vested equity at the time of sale.

*Rationale: Andres Montbrun's role is directly tied to increasing the operational value and commercial attractiveness of the company. This clause ensures his contribution to any realized exit is recognized regardless of vesting status at the time of sale.*

---

## 5. TERM AND CONDITIONS

- This agreement becomes effective upon signing by all parties below.
- Either party may terminate the management arrangement with **60 days written notice**.
- Upon termination for any reason other than cause, all vested equity tranches are retained by Andres Montbrun.
- Unvested tranches are forfeited upon voluntary resignation or termination for cause.
- This term sheet shall be formalized into a binding shareholders agreement within **30 days of signing.**
