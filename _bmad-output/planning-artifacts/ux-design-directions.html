Direction A: Clean Minimal

STRENGTHS
  • Maximum whitespace — professional, airy feel
  • WhatsApp as full-width bar — impossible to miss
  • Status indicator subtle — doesn't compete with hero
  • Service grid scannable with icon + label
  • Spec block with copper left border — clean data display
TRADE-OFFS
  • Hero headline below the photo — Carlos must scroll to see it
  • Subtle status might be missed on quick scan
  • No urgency signals above the fold
BEST FOR
  • Rodriguez (specs-focused, reads content)
  • James (professional, clean, English version)

Direction B: Bold Industrial

STRENGTHS
  • Status banner at very top — first thing seen, impossible to miss
  • Navy header + navy headline section = strong industrial identity
  • Copper used as section label accent — restrained but distinctive
  • WhatsApp full-width bar with no rounded corners — bold, urgent
  • Dark tab bar matches the navy theme — cohesive
  • Heritage line in copper uppercase — prominent without being large
TRADE-OFFS
  • Heavy use of navy — might feel dark on phone screens in sunlight
  • Status banner + navy header + photo + navy headline = a lot above fold before WhatsApp
  • Dark tab bar reduces contrast with content
BEST FOR
  • Carlos (bold, can't-miss WhatsApp, status is loud)
  • Creating strong brand differentiation from ALF

Direction C: Photo-Forward

STRENGTHS
  • Photo dominates — "these people are real" in the first second
  • Text overlaid on photo with gradient — cinematic, bold
  • Two clear path buttons (navy for galvanizing, copper for rods) — dual capability front and center
  • Big stat numbers (7m, 400) — scannable, impressive
  • Most visually distinctive of all directions
TRADE-OFFS
  • Photo overlay text may be hard to read if photo is busy
  • Large hero pushes WhatsApp below the fold on small phones
  • 360px hero = 80KB+ image budget risk
  • Gradient overlay adds visual complexity
BEST FOR
  • Making a strong first impression
  • Highlighting the dual-capability differentiator
  • Marketing / brand-building mood

Direction D: Card-Based

STRENGTHS
  • Contained photo (rounded corners) — feels modern, app-like
  • Status + capacity in a card — information grouped logically
  • Service list with descriptions — more informative than icon grid
  • List format with arrows — familiar mobile pattern (like Settings app)
  • Lighter image budget (180px hero vs 240px+)
TRADE-OFFS
  • Contained photo feels less industrial, more tech/app
  • List format is denser — less scannable than grid
  • Smaller hero = less visual impact on first load
  • Card-based design may feel too "startup" for industrial B2B
BEST FOR
  • Rodriguez (list format shows service details he cares about)
  • Information-dense approach that still feels clean

Direction E: Split Hero (Text First)

STRENGTHS
  • WhatsApp appears ABOVE the fold on ALL phones — Carlos's 5-second test passes guaranteed
  • Headline + status + WhatsApp all visible without scrolling
  • Photo is a reward for scrolling, not a gate
  • Three stat numbers (7m, 400, 936) show both capabilities at a glance
  • Outline buttons for dual path — lightweight, clean
  • Fastest time-to-WhatsApp of any direction
TRADE-OFFS
  • No photo above the fold — loses the "these people are real" instant visual proof
  • Text-first feels more like a tech landing page than an industrial site
  • Less emotional impact without the plant photo as first impression
BEST FOR
  • Carlos (fastest possible WhatsApp access)
  • Optimizing for conversion over brand impression
  • Fallback for very slow connections (text loads before images)

Direction F: Navy Immersive

STRENGTHS
  • Most distinctive visual identity — unmistakably PYGLARA
  • Navy-to-white transition (rounded corners) feels premium, app-like
  • Text + WhatsApp above fold before photo — conversion-optimized
  • Copper heritage line on dark background — pops beautifully
  • Green status on navy background — high contrast, visible
  • Strong brand differentiation from any competitor
TRADE-OFFS
  • Dark backgrounds use more battery on OLED phones (minor)
  • May feel too "tech startup" for traditional Venezuelan B2B
  • Outdoor sunlight readability on dark sections needs testing
  • More complex CSS than white-background approaches
BEST FOR
  • Maximum brand differentiation
  • Premium / modern positioning
  • Combining conversion (text-first) with identity (navy immersive)
