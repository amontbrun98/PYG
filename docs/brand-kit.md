## 1. Logo

### Files

| File | Format | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/logo-PG.jpeg` | JPEG | Square/stacked contexts: social profiles, stamps, badges |
| `assets/logo-prensados....jpeg` | JPEG | Horizontal contexts: letterheads, website header, printed materials |

### Usage Rules

- Always use the horizontal version (`logo-prensados`) for wide formats: website header, document headers, email signatures, printed materials
- Use the stacked version (`logo-PG`) for square crops: WhatsApp Business profile photo, Instagram profile, favicon source
- Minimum width for horizontal logo: 200px on screen, 40mm in print
- Clear space: maintain a margin equal to the height of the "P" on all sides — no text or graphics within that zone
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to either logo file
- Approved on: white background, light gray (#F5F5F5) background
- Do not place on dark backgrounds without a white-background container (a reversed white version does not currently exist)

### The Copper Y

In new digital and printed materials created from this point forward, when the full company name is written as running text (not as the logo), the letter **y** in "Prensados **y** Galvanizados" may be set in copper (#B87333) to make the Spanish "and" visually explicit. This applies to:
- Website headings where the full name appears
- Slide decks
- Printed capability statements

This is a typographic accent — it is not applied to the logo files themselves.

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary Colors

| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Navy** | `#1B3A5C` | 27, 58, 92 | Headings, nav, primary buttons, dominant color in all materials |
| **Copper** | `#B87333` | 184, 115, 51 | Accent only: the "y", stat units, thin rules under headings, icons. Never body text. |
| **Text** | `#1A1A1A` | 26, 26, 26 | All body text |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Page backgrounds, text on dark sections |
| **Light Gray** | `#F5F5F5` | 245, 245, 245 | Alternating section backgrounds |

### Secondary Colors

| Name | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Green** | `#2D8B4E` | 45, 139, 78 | Operational status indicators, active states only |
| **Amber** | `#D4A017` | 212, 160, 23 | "Pending" / "coming soon" states — large text only |
| **WhatsApp Green** | `#25D366` | 37, 211, 102 | WhatsApp CTA buttons only — not a brand color |

### WCAG Contrast Ratios

| Combination | Ratio | Grade | Safe for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Navy on White | 10.3:1 | AAA | All text sizes |
| Text (#1A1A1A) on White | 16.6:1 | AAA | All text sizes |
| Green on White | 4.6:1 | AA | Body text and above |
| Copper on White | 3.5:1 | Fails AA | Large text (24px+), icons, decorative only |
| Amber on White | 2.8:1 | Fails AA | Decorative only — never text |
| White on Navy | 10.3:1 | AAA | All text sizes |

**Hard rule:** Copper and Amber are never used for body text, form labels, captions, or any text below 24px. Violation breaks WCAG 2.1 AA compliance required for international clients.

### Grayscale Behavior (B&W Print)

| Color | Grayscale value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Navy #1B3A5C | ~20% luminance — very dark | Strong contrast on white |
| Copper #B87333 | ~45% luminance — medium gray | Distinguishable from navy |
| Green #2D8B4E | ~30% luminance — dark-medium | Similar to copper — avoid pairing in B&W contexts |
| Text #1A1A1A | ~7% luminance — near black | Maximum legibility |
| Light Gray #F5F5F5 | ~96% luminance — near white | Barely visible as section separator in B&W |

All materials must remain fully legible when photocopied in black and white.

### Physical Validation Required

Before final lock on any new campaign materials:
- [ ] Print a test page (color + B&W) on a standard office printer
- [ ] Check on a budget Android phone (Samsung A-series or Xiaomi Redmi) in outdoor daylight
- [ ] Confirm copper and amber elements are decorative and not relied upon for information

## 5. Photography

### Rules

- Real plant photos only. No stock imagery. Ever.
- Phone quality (iPhone or Android) is acceptable — no professional photoshoot required
- No heavy filters, color grading, or Instagram-style processing. Natural light.
- No logos or watermarks added over photos (the @pyglarasa Instagram handle visible in the background is fine)

### Walk-Through Sequence (8-10 photos)

The photo set should simulate a plant visit in this order:

1. Plant exterior / entrance (Zona Industrial I, Barquisimeto)
2. Warehouse overview — full bay, overhead cranes visible
3. 7m Pilling kettle — even if empty, show the scale
4. Kettle bay detail — burners, zinc loading area
5. 65cm centrifuge kettle (active — galvanizing nails)
6. Copper electroplating line
7. Overhead crane closeup
8. Industrial scale / measurement equipment
9. Finished galvanized product (pletinas, nails, hardware)
10. Secondary warehouse / storage area

## 6. Tone of Voice

### Principles

**Real over polished.** PYGLARA has operated for 36 years, survived Venezuela's economic crisis, and maintained equipment that competitors abandoned. The story is the proof. Don't embellish it — state it.

**Numbers are the design.** "300 TM/mes", "17,280 TM/año", "34 clientes históricos", "2 plantas activas en todo el país". These are not supporting details — they are the headline.

**Active and direct.** "Galvanizamos." Not "Galvanizing solutions are provided." "Solo hay 2 plantas activas en Venezuela." Not "PYGLARA is among the very few active galvanizing operations."

**No superlatives without evidence.** "La única planta con cobre electrolítico en Venezuela" is verifiable — use it. "Servicio de clase mundial" is not — never use it.

**Bilingual, not translated.** Spanish is primary. English is peer-quality, adapted for an international industrial buyer, not a literal word-for-word translation of the Spanish.

### Voice by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial outreach (clients) | Direct, peer-to-peer, industrial | "Estamos a 200 metros. Cuándo puede visitarnos." |
| Investor materials | Precise, data-forward, institutional | "At conservative 50 TM/month, EBITDA margin reaches 41%." |
| Website | Professional, accessible, bilingual | "Zinc que protege. Cobre que conduce." |
| WhatsApp | Conversational but professional | "Buenos días, somos PYGLARA. Recibimos su consulta..." |

## 9. Investor Document Visual Standards

### Page Setup

- Paper: Carta / Letter (8.5 × 11 in / 216 × 279mm)
- Margins: 25mm all sides
- Target page count: 25-35 pages per language version

### Color Application

- Navy (#1B3A5C) dominant: headings, chart bars, section structure
- Copper (#B87333) sparingly: thin accent line under section headings, bullet markers, chart secondary series
- No backgrounds in copper or amber — text and white only as backgrounds
- No gradients, no shadows, no decorative elements, no clip art

### Charts and Data

- Flat bar charts, no 3D effects
- Navy as primary chart color, copper as secondary/accent series only
- Data labels directly on charts — no separate legends
- Muted grid lines

### Headers / Footers

- Header: company logo (horizontal) small in top-right corner
- Footer left: "PYGLARA — Confidencial" (ES) / "PYGLARA — Confidential" (EN)
- Footer right: page number
- Contact info on cover and final page only
