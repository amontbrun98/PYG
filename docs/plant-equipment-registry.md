**Note (2026-03-25):** Ing. Miriam confirmed PYGLARA operates under COVENIN 1212-81 AND international ASTM A123/A153. This is stronger than previously documented (only ASTM B499 for thickness measurement). ASTM A123 is the definitive international standard for hot-dip galvanizing quality — this makes PYGLARA export-grade compliant.
