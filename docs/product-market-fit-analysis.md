## SALES APPROACH BY ACTIVATION PHASE (Revised — Capital Efficiency Order)

### Phase 0 — NOW (65cm only)
- Sell galvanized nails, bolts, small hardware to ferreterias and distributors
- Build client relationships and cash flow

### Phase 1 — COPPER LINE FIRST ($15-30K) — Highest ROI
- Target: electrical contractors, distributors, telecom, CORPOELEC, PDVSA
- Product: copper-coated ground rods
- Revenue potential: $561K-$1.05M/year at 25% capacity
- Use copper cash flow to fund Phase 2

### Phase 2 — 3m kettle (~$70K, funded by copper revenue)
- Target: solar installers, highway contractors, ferreterias, fabrication shops
- Products: cable trays, solar mounts, crash barriers, earthing strips
- Cross-sell to copper clients

### Phase 3 — 7m kettle (~$180K, funded by copper + 3m revenue)
- Target: PDVSA, CORPOELEC, telecom, construction companies
- Products: tower sections, structural steel, substation structures, pipe supports
- 400 tons/month capacity, $2.16M/year revenue at full capacity
- Raise investor capital here at higher valuation (proven operations, revenue history)

### Phase 4 — 9m kettle (investor capital)
- Target: mega-projects, ports, large transmission, high mast
- Unlocks oversized work no regional competitor can match
