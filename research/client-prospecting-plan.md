## PHASE 1 — COPPER ELECTROPLATING (Investment: $15-30K)

**Product:** Copper-coated ground rods (varillas de puesta a tierra cobreadas)
- 5/8" to 1" diameter, 1.2m to 3m length, 300um copper coating
- Capacity: 936 rods/day

**Why copper first:** Lowest capital, highest ROI, immediate cash flow. Ground rods are a consumable — clients reorder regularly.

### Target Clients

**A. Electrical Contractors (Contratistas Electricos)**
These companies install grounding systems for every building, industrial plant, and substation they work on. Ground rods are a recurring purchase.

- [ ] Electrical contractors in Barquisimeto (Yellow pages / Colegio de Ingenieros de Lara)
- [ ] Electrical contractors in Valencia, Maracaibo, Caracas
- [ ] Companies specializing in lightning protection (pararrayos)

**B. Electrical Distributors (Distribuidores de Material Electrico)**
Sell in bulk to these distributors and let them handle retail.

- [ ] Ferreterias industriales in Zona Industrial I, Barquisimeto
- [ ] Electrical supply houses (casas de materiales electricos) in Lara state
- [ ] National electrical distributors with warehouse networks

**C. Telecom Companies**
Every cell tower and telecom installation requires grounding systems.

- [ ] Movilnet — procurement / infrastructure department
- [ ] Digitel — network expansion teams
- [ ] Movistar — site contractors
- [ ] Tower companies (torreeras) that build and maintain cell sites

**D. CORPOELEC / Power Utilities**
Substations and transmission infrastructure require extensive grounding.

- [ ] CORPOELEC — western region procurement (Barquisimeto)
- [ ] Substation construction contractors
- [ ] Power plant maintenance contractors

**E. Oil & Gas**
Refineries, pumping stations, and pipeline installations all need grounding.

- [ ] PDVSA — procurement department
- [ ] Oil service companies (Schlumberger, Halliburton, Baker Hughes local offices)
- [ ] Pipeline contractors in Zulia, Falcon, Lara
- [ ] Refinery maintenance contractors (CRP - Centro de Refinacion Paraguaná)

**F. Construction Companies**
New commercial and industrial buildings require grounding systems per electrical code.

- [ ] Large construction companies in Lara state
- [ ] Industrial park developers
- [ ] Data center builders

**G. Mining Operations**
Mining sites require extensive grounding for safety.

- [ ] CVG operations (Guayana)
- [ ] Private mining companies (gold, iron, bauxite)

### Copper Sales Pitch
- Only manufacturer in western Venezuela (no local competition)
- Per-lot quality certificates with thickness verification (ASTM B499)
- PYGLARA brand with 50 years of trajectory
- Competitive pricing vs. imported alternatives
- Immediate availability / short lead times
- Can produce custom lengths and diameters
